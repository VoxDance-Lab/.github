# VoxDance Lab


VoxDance Lab is an independent research lab focused on advancing audio AI and generative modeling.

We explore new paradigms in speech generation, continuous acoustic representations, speech understanding, and multimodal modeling. Our goal is to push the boundaries of audio intelligence through fundamental research and open-source development.

---

## 🔬 Research Areas

- **Speech Generation** — Autoregressive TTS, zero-shot voice cloning, and expressive speech synthesis.
- **Continuous Acoustic Representations** — Exploring new representations and modeling paradigms for high-fidelity audio generation.
- **Speech Understanding & Interaction** — Speech-to-speech modeling and real-time spoken interaction.
- **Multimodal Modeling** — Bridging audio, visual, and linguistic modalities.

---

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### VoxMel — Wavelet-Factorized Autoregressive TTS

A tokenizer-free autoregressive TTS framework that explores direct modeling of continuous mel-spectrograms through fixed, invertible wavelet factorization.

**Key Features**
- Zero-shot voice cloning
- Cross-lingual speech generation
- Expressive English and Chinese speech
- Continuous autoregressive acoustic modeling

**Model Sizes:** 0.3B / 0.6B

- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/VoxDance-Lab/VoxMel)
- [Demo](https://voxdance.github.io/voxmel-demo/)
